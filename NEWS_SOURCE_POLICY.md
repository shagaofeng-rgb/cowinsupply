# CowinSupply News Source Policy

## Eligible sources

News candidates must be English, publicly accessible, dated within 90 days, and relevant to professional construction, industrial tools, HVAC/MEP, metalwork, maintenance, finishing or measurement workflows. Priority is given to official government, standards, associations, event organizers, manufacturer newsrooms and high-trust professional industry media.

The initial V2 source registry contains the public Construction Dive news RSS feed as a high-trust professional industry-media source. Administrators can replace or supplement this registry in the News control panel/API. Each source record stores publisher, RSS URL, type, trust level, language, enable state and license note.

## Exclusions

The system rejects undated material, sources over 90 days old, blacklisted sources, SEO-content farms, scraped pages, anonymous pages, social-only claims, gambling/adult/illegal content, inaccessible source URLs and items without meaningful verified product relevance.

## Attribution and image rules

The source URL, publisher, author when available, publication date, fetch time, region and SHA-256 source fingerprint are retained. The original source is linked on the published page. The system writes original analysis; it does not reproduce the source article.

No third-party editorial image is downloaded, copied or hotlinked. A publishable News item must have a CowinSupply-owned product image from the live product record, with the image-license note saved alongside the article.
