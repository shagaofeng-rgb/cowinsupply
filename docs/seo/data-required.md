# Product Data Required Before Indexing

For every product intended for indexing, maintain real, reviewable information:

1. Product model and manufacturer confirmation.
2. Accurate title, buyer-facing summary and category.
3. At least one verified specification row.
4. Current product image with descriptive alt text.
5. Application and material-use context.
6. Voltage, configuration, accessory and compliance details where relevant.
7. A truthful SEO title and description; do not state factory ownership, certification, pricing or performance claims without evidence.

Products missing these fields can remain available for enquiry but should have `seoIndexable: false` until reviewed.
